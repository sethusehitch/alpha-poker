# Summit replacement acceptance protocol

Local experiment started 2026-09-08 at approximately 21:08 UTC. No production changes authorized by this experiment. Current Mirage and Summit remain frozen pending all gates.

1. Screen three existing research candidates in actual first-to-three-games PLHE series. Choose development starting point using Mirage, current Summit, balanced value, and small pressure. These small samples are exploratory, not confirmation. The range prototype had the best total screen (15/20 versus 11/20 middle and equity result saved separately).
2. Give a builder approximately 30 minutes of focused evidence-driven improvement, with a versioned directory and independent strategy RNG. The builder may view development results only. No source/seed/opponent-identity tricks. Deal reconstruction and external bot/process/file inspection are prohibited.
3. Freeze candidate source and package hash before confirmation. Two fresh disjoint batches of 20 series versus unchanged Mirage must EACH yield at least 18 wins. Balance seats 10/10. Save every outcome; never rerun failed seeds as a substitute. Any strategy change invalidates both confirmations and requires new seeds. A failed candidate may inform development, but its used seeds are no longer holdouts.
4. Lower packaged bots must each be beaten at least 18/20 series on fresh deals. Compare current Summit as well. Run all 11 round-two reference opponents and compare to frozen original range policy under matched deal/strategy-seed schedules. Require no decline in aggregate reference wins and investigate any per-opponent drop exceeding two wins out of ten with larger independent tests before claiming no regression. This is an operational regression gate, not a statistical proof of noninferiority or general poker strength.
5. Require zero bot errors/forfeits, real sandbox loading, bounded legal actions on stack/blind/street edge cases, source audit, and preservation of any vendor licenses. Fresh processes per series, memory retained across games in that series. Independent strategy RNG is generated separately from deals and recorded by the harness; this prevents validation from depending on the existing runtime's deal-derived random seed field. The gameplay rules remain unchanged.
6. Only after passing may the candidate replace Summit locally in a separately isolated integration checkout. Preserve Mirage and the other packaged policies. Test both server and local CLI packaging, existing train/viewer behavior, and source parity. No public Elo changes, release, or difficulty-time promises.

Evidence distinguishes tournament series, games, and hands. No cherry-picked hand profits, mirrored-cash results, or re-used tuning deals count as confirmation. Passing the matchup gates does not prove an hour-to-beat learning difficulty; fresh independent student-agent testing is a separate task.
